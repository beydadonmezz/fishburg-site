Privacy Policy

Fishburg: Aquarium · Effective date: 15 October 2026

The short version

  • Fishburg has no sign-up: no email, no password, no name. Your save game is stored on your device, and deleting the app deletes it.
  • For the friends feature, the game creates an anonymous account with a random player ID and a generated display name when it first starts. More is stored on our server (your level, a copy of how your tanks look, friends, likes, blocks and reports) only after you join Friends and accept its rules.
  • You can delete the social account at any time in the game's Settings. We don't use this data for ads and we don't sell it.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. They are personalised only if you agree (in Apple's tracking prompt and, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, also in the consent form); otherwise you get non-personalised ads. Either way, Google collects some device and usage data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.
  • Purchases and Game Center are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • The game works fully whatever you choose in any privacy prompt.

This policy covers the Fishburg game for iPhone and iPad, listed on the App Store as Fishburg: Aquarium, and this website.

1. Who we are

Fishburg is made by BEYDANUR DONMEZ (Beydanur Dönmez) ("we", "us"). We are responsible (the "data controller") for how the Fishburg app handles personal data. You can reach us at fishburg.sandbox@outlook.com.

Google and Apple provide some services inside the app (ads, purchases, Game Center). They handle the data they collect under their own privacy policies, which are linked below. Supabase hosts the friends feature for us and handles that data only on our instructions. Expo passes the notifications about friends on to Apple's push service for us (see Notifications).

2. What stays on your device

You can play Fishburg without an internet connection. The following is stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone or iPad. Apart from the copy of your tanks and your level described under Friends and your social account, it is never sent to us:

  • your tanks, fish, decorations, coins, gems, level, awards, daily tasks and calendar progress (your save game);
  • your settings, such as language, sound, music and notifications;
  • which purchases you have made (for example, Remove Ads or the Starter Pack), so the game can apply them.

We do not use our own analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools. We have no copy of your save: your coins, gems, purchases, awards and the names of your fish and tanks never leave your device.

3. Friends and your social account

Fishburg has a friends feature. You add other players with a player ID, look at their tanks, leave a like and clean their glass. It runs on a server that we rent from Supabase (see Who stores it). This is the only part of Fishburg where we keep data about you.

Created when the game first starts

The first time Fishburg starts with an internet connection, it creates an anonymous social account for the game on your device. You give no email address, no password, no name and no Apple Account, and we do not learn who you are. At that point the server stores only:

  • a random account ID and the time the account was created;
  • a public player ID in the form FB-XXXX-XXXX;
  • a display name that the game generates for you;
  • your app language, so that text sent to you can be in your language;
  • your device's time zone (a name such as Europe/Istanbul, taken from your device's settings), used only so that notifications about friends follow the quiet hours and the daily limit in your local time (see Notifications).

Nothing else is sent until you join Friends. Until then no other player can find you, even with your player ID.

The first time you use Friends, the game shows the rules and asks you to accept them. If you don't accept, nothing below is sent. After you accept, the server also stores:

A friend can change one thing in your game: when a friend cleans your glass, the server notes when it happened, and your game then wipes the glass clean on your device. The friend gets a small coin reward in their own game. You get no coins for it, and a friend can change nothing else.

Who can see it

  • Your display name, player ID and level can be seen by anyone who enters your player ID in the game.
  • The copy of your tanks can be seen only by friends you have accepted.
  • Players you have blocked see nothing of yours.
  • Reports are seen only by us.

There is no chat and there are no messages. The only text that other players can read from you is your display name.

What we use it for

We use this data only to run the friends feature and to keep it safe (for example, to review reports and remove offensive names). We do not use it for advertising or tracking, we do not sell it, and we share it with nobody except the providers named below: the hosting provider and, for notifications about friends, the push services.

Who stores it

The data is stored by Supabase (Supabase, Inc.), which hosts the database and the anonymous sign-in for us and acts only on our instructions (as our "data processor"). The database is in Supabase's Central EU region in Frankfurt, Germany (Amazon Web Services region eu-central-1). Like any web server, Supabase's servers also see your IP address in ordinary request logs when the game talks to them, and the sign-in service uses it to limit how many new accounts can be created from one address in an hour. See the Supabase Privacy Policy and the Supabase Data Processing Addendum.

Notifications about friends are sent by our server to the push service of Expo (650 Industries, Inc., United States), which passes them to the Apple Push Notification service, and Apple delivers them to your device. For this, Expo and Apple receive your device's push token and the text of the notification, which contains the display name of the other player (a notification that groups several events gives only their number). See the Expo Privacy Policy.

Deleting your social account

In the game, open Settings → Delete social account. This removes the account from the server at once, together with everything listed above. Your game on the device is not touched. Two things to know:

  • Reports. Reports are the one thing that outlives the account, for safety review. A report that another player filed about you loses its link to your account and keeps only your player ID and display name as plain text. A report that you filed loses its link to you. We review reports, and every report is deleted 12 months after it was filed.
  • No new account. After deletion the game stays signed out of Friends, and no new account is created. If you later open Friends and choose to join again, you get a brand-new anonymous account with a new player ID.

If you can no longer open the app, email fishburg.sandbox@outlook.com with your player ID and we will delete the account for you.

Reports and moderation

You can report a player whose player ID you have (a friend, or someone who sent you a friend request) from inside the game, once per player per day, and you can block players. We review reports ourselves. We remove offensive names and delete the accounts of abusive players, and we aim to act within 24 hours of a report. The rules are in the Terms of Use.

4. Ads by Google AdMob

Fishburg is free to play and is funded by ads. Fishburg shows ads from Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK): rewarded videos that you choose to watch (for example, to revive a fish or to collect the Free Gift), and an occasional full-screen ad after a level-up. There are no banner ads. Ads in Fishburg are limited to content rated PG (Google's "parental guidance" rating). Fishburg is not directed at children.

When you first open Fishburg, Google's consent tool asks for your choices: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form; everywhere, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt (after a short explanation). Ads are loaded only after this step. The details are under Your privacy choices.

Personalised ads, only if you agreed

You get personalised ads only if you agreed: you chose "Allow" in Apple's tracking prompt and, if you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you also agreed to personalised ads in the consent form.

With personalised ads, Google may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and information about the ads you see and how you use the app to show ads based on your interests and to measure them, also across other companies' apps and websites. Apple calls this "tracking", which is why we ask first.

Non-personalised ads in every other case

In every other case (you chose "Ask App Not to Track", you have not answered, tracking is restricted on your device, or you refused personalised ads in the consent form), Fishburg asks Google for non-personalised ads only.

Non-personalised ads are not based on your past behaviour, but Google still uses some data to deliver them: your IP address for approximate (city-level) location and to prevent fraud and invalid activity; device or app-specific identifiers to limit how often you see the same ad and for aggregated ad reporting; the ads shown to you and your taps on them; and crash and performance data. Without your permission in the tracking prompt, iOS does not give the advertising identifier to Fishburg or Google.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, if you do not consent, Google may show "limited ads" that use no data for personalisation.

Fishburg never reads your advertising identifier itself and never gives Google your Fishburg account or player ID.

5. Your privacy choices

Before any ad loads, Google's consent tool shows the privacy messages that apply to you:

  • Consent form (EEA, UK and Switzerland). You can agree to or refuse personalised ads and the use of your device's storage and identifiers. The form has a "Do not consent" button. If you refuse personalised ads, Fishburg asks Google for non-personalised ads only; if you do not consent, Google may show "limited ads" that use no data for personalisation.
  • Tracking prompt (everywhere). Google first shows a short message explaining why we ask (in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the consent form can take its place), and then Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether Fishburg may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Allow, ads can be personalised (in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland only if you also agreed in the consent form). If you choose Ask App Not to Track, iOS does not give your advertising identifier (IDFA) to Fishburg or Google, and Fishburg asks for non-personalised ads only.

You can change your choice at any time:

  • Tracking: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Fishburg (on iPad, the same path in iPad Settings). Fishburg checks your tracking choice each time you return to the game, and the next ad follows it. More about tracking permission from Apple.
  • Ad consent, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: also Fishburg → Settings → Privacy options, which opens the consent form again.
  • Google ad personalisation in general: My Ad Center.

The game works the same whatever you choose, and no reward depends on it. You can still play everything and still choose to watch rewarded ads.

6. In-app purchases

Purchases (gem packs, coin packs, the Starter Pack and Remove Ads) are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We never receive your name, email, card or other payment details. Apple only tells the app that a purchase succeeded, with a transaction number, so the game can apply it on your device. Apple handles this data under the Apple Privacy Policy. Refunds are handled by Apple. See the Terms of Use for what each purchase includes.

7. Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, Fishburg sends your total XP to a leaderboard and reports achievements (one for each award tier you reach). Your Game Center nickname and score can be seen by other players on the leaderboard, according to your Game Center settings. Apple processes this data under Game Center & Privacy. We don't receive or store your Game Center data outside of what Apple shows on the leaderboard.

Game Center is optional and the game works fully without it. You can sign out at any time in iOS Settings → Game Center.

8. Notifications

Fishburg can remind you when a fish is hungry or about to starve, when an egg has hatched, when the glass needs cleaning and when your Free Gift is ready. These are local notifications: they are scheduled on your device by the game itself, from your save, and nothing is sent to us for them. You get at most three reminders a day and none between 22:00 and 09:00. The game asks for permission at the end of the tutorial (and at most once more later, if a fish has starved), and you can turn reminders off, all of them or one kind at a time, in the game settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications → Fishburg.

Notifications about friends are push notifications. If you have joined Friends, the Notifications switch in the game's Settings is on and you have already allowed notifications for Fishburg, the game registers a push token for your device and stores it with your social account on our server (see Stored after you join Friends). The game shows no extra permission prompt for this: it uses the notification permission it already asks for. You then get notifications when you receive a friend request, when a friend request of yours is accepted, when someone likes your tank and when a friend cleans your glass. Our server applies limits like those of the reminders, counted in your local time from your device's time zone (UTC until the server knows it):

  • none between 22:00 and 09:00; what arrives at night is sent after 09:00 as one notification;
  • events are gathered for a minute, and after a notification nothing is sent for 30 minutes, so several events become one notification (for example "You have 3 new updates from friends"). A notification therefore arrives a minute or two after the event, not at once;
  • at most three notifications about friends a day, in addition to the up to three reminders;
  • an event that could not be sent within 12 hours is not sent any more.

A notification about a single event begins with the other player's display name; a grouped one gives only the number of updates. The text is in the language of your account. Our server sends it to Expo's push service, which passes it to the Apple Push Notification service (see Who stores it). The Inbox in Friends shows every event, including those that were not sent as a notification.

To stop notifications about friends on a device, switch off Notifications in the game's Settings (the switch for all reminders; the switches for single kinds of reminder do not affect them). The game then removes that device's push token from our server (if the device is offline, as soon as it is online again), and registers it again when you switch Notifications back on. Turning notifications off in iOS Settings → Notifications → Fishburg also stops them, together with the reminders. Deleting your social account deletes all push tokens.

9. Children and age rating

Fishburg is rated 9+ on the App Store and is not in the Kids category. It is a general-audience game, not directed at children under 13, and we do not ask for anyone's age. The friends feature has no chat, no messages and no photos: the only text players can see from each other is a display name, which passes a filter for offensive words, and the social account holds no name, email address or other contact details. If you are a parent and want your child's social account removed, use Settings → Delete social account in the game or email us with the player ID. Ads are limited to content rated PG, and they are personalised only if the user chose "Allow" in Apple's tracking prompt (and, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, also agreed in the consent form); in every other case they are non-personalised (see Ads by Google AdMob). If you are a parent and have a question, please contact us.

10. Keeping and deleting data

  • Your save game stays on your device until you delete the app. Deleting Fishburg removes the save and your settings. A copy may remain in your own iCloud or computer backups until those backups are replaced.
  • Your social account and the friends data are kept on the server until you delete the account (Settings → Delete social account) or until we close the friends feature. Deletion takes effect at once. Reports are deleted 12 months after they were filed; if an account in a report is deleted earlier, the report loses its link to that account, as described under Deleting your social account. The inbox keeps only the last 100 events. Push tokens are deleted with the account, or earlier when you switch Notifications off in the game on that device or when Apple or Expo reports that the device is no longer registered. Supabase keeps its request logs (which include IP addresses) for a limited time under its own rules.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as we need to answer them, and at most two years.
  • Google keeps ad data for the periods described in How Google retains data. You can withdraw ad consent at any time (see Your privacy choices).
  • Apple keeps purchase and Game Center data under its own policy.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights over your personal data. For the friends data you can do most things yourself: change your display name in the game, and delete the whole social account in Settings → Delete social account. For a copy of the data, or for anything else, email us and include your player ID (we have no other way to find your account). Requests about ad data are best handled by Google directly, but we're happy to help.

Turkey (KVKK)

Under Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data, you can learn whether your data is processed, ask for information about it and its purpose, learn which third parties it is transferred to in Turkey or abroad, ask for correction or deletion, object to results based solely on automated processing, and claim compensation for unlawful processing. We process the friends data because it is needed to provide a feature you asked for and, for the anonymous account and for moderation, on the basis of our legitimate interest in running the feature safely. You can apply to us first and then complain to the Personal Data Protection Board. A Turkish version of this policy is available.

EEA and UK (GDPR)

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, and receive it in a portable format. Where we rely on consent (for example, for personalised ads), you can withdraw it at any time without affecting earlier use. Otherwise ads rely on our legitimate interest in funding a free game, limited to non-personalised or limited ads, fraud prevention and security. The friends data is processed to provide the feature you joined (performance of a contract); creating the anonymous account and reviewing reports rely on our legitimate interest in running the feature and keeping it safe. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell personal information for money, and we neither sell nor share the friends data. Where the use of your advertising identifier for personalised ads counts as "sharing" under California law, you can opt out by choosing Ask App Not to Track (Fishburg then asks Google for non-personalised ads only) and, where the game offers it, in Settings → Privacy options. You also have the right to know about and delete personal information, and you will not be treated differently for using your rights.

13. International transfers and security

Google, Apple and GitHub may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, using the safeguards described in their privacy policies. Expo (650 Industries, Inc.) is a company in the United States: for notifications about friends it receives your device's push token, the random installation ID described under Stored after you join Friends and the notification text (with another player's display name, or only a number of updates) and passes them on to Apple, under the Expo Privacy Policy.

The friends data is stored in Frankfurt, Germany, in a Supabase data centre run on Amazon Web Services (region eu-central-1). It is in the European Union, not in Turkey, and it may be outside the country where you live. Supabase is a company based in the United States, and it and its sub-processors may also handle data in the United States and other countries, for example for support and to operate the service. Supabase's Data Processing Addendum includes the European Commission's standard contractual clauses for such transfers. The connection between the game and the server is encrypted, and other players can read only what is listed under Who can see it.

On your device, your save is protected by iOS app sandboxing and your device's security.

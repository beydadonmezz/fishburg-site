7. Friends and player conduct

Friends is a part of the game where you add other players with a player ID, look at their tanks, leave a like and clean their glass. The game creates an anonymous social account for it, with a player ID and a generated display name, when it first starts. You join Friends by accepting its rules in the game. From then on your display name, player ID and level can be seen by anyone who enters your player ID, and a copy of your tanks can be seen by the friends you accept. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what is stored.

We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. When you use Friends, you agree that you will not:

  • use a display name that is offensive, hateful, sexual, violent, threatening or otherwise objectionable, or that contains someone's personal details or pretends to be another person, us or Apple;
  • harass, bully or threaten other players, including through repeated friend requests or through false reports;
  • try to get around the name filter, a block or the removal of an account;
  • use scripts, bots or modified versions of the app with Friends, or interfere with the server.
  • Name filter. Display names are checked by a filter for offensive words. A name that passes the filter can still break these rules.
  • Report and block. From inside the game you can report a player whose player ID you have (a friend, or someone who sent you a friend request) and block players. A blocked player sees nothing of yours. You can also write to fishburg.sandbox@outlook.com with the player ID of the player you want to report.
  • What we do. We review every report. We remove objectionable names and delete the social accounts of players who break these rules, and we aim to act within 24 hours of a report. We can do this without warning. A removed account loses its friends list, likes and other friends data. Your game on your device and your purchases are not affected.
  • Leaving. You can delete your social account at any time with Settings → Delete social account. The game on your device stays as it is.
  • Cleaning a friend's glass. When a friend cleans your glass, the glass in your own game becomes clean too. The friend gets a small coin reward (once per friend per day, for at most ten cleanings a day). You get no coins for glass that a friend cleaned.
  • No value. Likes, friends and anything else in Friends have no real-world value and cannot be exchanged for money.

The server applies daily limits, for example on friend requests, likes, glass cleanings, reports and name changes, and a limit on how many new accounts can be created from one internet address in an hour. Friends needs an internet connection and our server. It may be unavailable at times, and we may change it or close it. The rest of the game does not depend on it.

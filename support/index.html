Support

Need a hand with your aquarium? Most answers are below. If not, just write to us.

Contact us

Email fishburg.sandbox@outlook.com. It helps if you include:

  • your iPhone or iPad model and iOS/iPadOS version;
  • the Fishburg version and build (shown at the bottom of Settings in the game);
  • what happened, and a screenshot if you can.

For purchase problems, the order ID from Apple's receipt email is useful. Never send us card details or your Apple Account password. We try to reply within a few working days.

Frequently asked questions

Progress and saving

Where is my game saved?

Only on your device. Fishburg has no sign-up and no cloud save, and we don't keep a copy of your save. (If you join Friends, the server holds a read-only copy of how your tanks look so your friends can see them. It is not a backup.) Your aquarium keeps "living" while the app is closed: fish grow and get hungry, and the glass slowly gets dirty, in real time.

I deleted the app or got a new device. Can I get my progress back?

Deleting the app deletes the save. If you restore your device from an iCloud or computer backup made before you deleted the app, or move to a new device by transferring your data, the save comes with it. Because we have no copy on a server, we can't restore progress for you. We're sorry about that.

Will changing my device's clock speed things up?

No. The game notices when the clock goes backwards and doesn't grant extra progress. It's best to leave the date and time set automatically.

Purchases

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases in the game, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy. This restores Remove Ads. Gem packs, coin packs and the Starter Pack are used up when you buy them: their gems and coins live in your save and can't be restored.

I paid but didn't get my gems or coins.

Make sure you're online, close Fishburg completely and open it again. The game finishes any pending purchase when it starts. If they still don't appear, email us with the order ID from Apple's receipt.

Where did the Starter Pack go?

The Starter Pack can be bought once per game save. After you buy it, it disappears from the Store.

How do I get a refund?

Payments go through Apple, so refunds do too. You can request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Game Center

How do the leaderboard and achievements work?

If you're signed in to Game Center, your total XP goes to the Fishburg leaderboard and each award tier you reach unlocks an achievement. Tap the Game Center button at the bottom right of the main screen to see them.

How do I sign in or out of Game Center?

Go to iOS Settings → Game Center. The game works fully without Game Center; your awards are still saved on your device.

Notifications

How do the reminders work?

Fishburg can remind you when a fish is hungry, when a fish is about to starve, when an egg has hatched, when the glass needs cleaning and when your Free Gift is ready. You get at most three reminders a day and none between 22:00 and 09:00. Reminders are created on your device by the game, and no server is involved. Notifications about friends work differently (see the next question).

Do I get notifications about friends?

Yes, if you have joined Friends and have already allowed notifications for Fishburg. You get one when you receive a friend request, when a friend request of yours is accepted, when someone likes your tank and when a friend cleans your glass. The game shows no extra permission prompt for this. These are push notifications: the game stores a push token for your device with your social account, and our server sends the text through Expo's push service and Apple's push service. The reminder limits (three a day, none at night) do not apply to them. The Inbox in Friends shows the same events either way. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Use the notification switches in the game's Settings (one for all reminders and one for each kind), or go to iOS Settings → Notifications → Fishburg. Your fish still get hungry with reminders off, so check in now and then or use Vacation Mode. To stop notifications about friends, turn notifications off in iOS Settings → Notifications → Fishburg; that turns the reminders off too.

Friends

What is my player ID, and what can other players see?

Your player ID looks like FB-XXXX-XXXX and is shown on the Friends screen. Another player needs it to send you a friend request. Anyone who enters it sees your display name and level. Only friends you have accepted can look at your tanks. Fishburg has no chat. The Privacy Policy has the full list of what is stored.

What happens when a friend cleans my glass?

Your glass becomes clean in your own game too, the next time your game is online. Your friend gets a small coin reward: once per friend per day, for at most ten cleanings a day. You get no coins for glass that a friend cleaned. You see who cleaned it in the Inbox in Friends, and you get a notification about it if notifications are allowed for Fishburg.

How do I report a player?

Open that player in Friends (a friend, or someone who sent you a friend request) and choose Report, pick a reason and add a note if you like. You can report the same player once a day. We review every report, remove offensive names and delete abusive accounts, and we aim to act within 24 hours. You can also email fishburg.sandbox@outlook.com with the player's ID and what happened.

How do I block a player?

Open that player in Friends and choose Block. A blocked player sees nothing of yours: no name, no level and no tanks.

How do I delete my social account?

In the game, open Settings → Delete social account. The account and everything stored with it on the server (player ID, display name, level, the copy of your tanks, friends, likes, blocks, inbox and push tokens) are removed at once. Your aquarium on your device is not touched. No new account is created afterwards. If you later open Friends and join again, you get a brand-new account with a new player ID.

I no longer have the app. Can you delete my social account?

Yes. Email fishburg.sandbox@outlook.com with your player ID (FB-XXXX-XXXX) and ask us to delete the account. The player ID is the only way we can find it, because the account holds no name or email address.

Ads and privacy

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Rewarded ads are always your choice. You might occasionally see a full-screen ad after a level-up; Remove Ads turns those off.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the game, open Settings → Privacy options (shown where a consent choice applies, such as in the EEA and UK). For the tracking permission, go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Gameplay

My fish died while I was away. What can I do?

A fish dies only after it has been hungry for 48 hours in a row. How soon a fish gets hungry after a meal depends on its species (between 6 and 24 hours). If reminders are on, the game warns you before a fish starves. Tap a dead fish to revive it with coins, with gems or by watching a rewarded ad. It comes back at the stage it was in. Before a long break, use Vacation Mode (from the Shop) to pause hunger, growth and glass dirt in a tank for three days.

How do I clean the glass?

Tap the sponge and drag your finger across the dirty glass. You earn coins for the area you clean.

How do I change the language?

Fishburg follows your device's language. You can pick a different one in Settings → Language.

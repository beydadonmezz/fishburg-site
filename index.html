Fishburg

A cozy aquarium that keeps swimming while you're away. Hatch fish from eggs, keep them fed, polish the glass and build the tanks of your dreams.

Coming soon to the App Store as “Fishburg: Aquarium” for iPhone and iPad
  • From egg to adultBuy an egg, place it in the water and watch it hatch. Fish grow through five stages, and each stage is worth more.
  • Keep them happySprinkle food when a hunger bubble appears. Fish get hungry in real time, so check in now and then.
  • A sparkling tankThe glass slowly gets grubby. Grab the sponge and wipe it clean for a few extra coins.
  • Decorate your wayCorals, ruins, treasure and little critters that wander the sand. Move, flip and resize everything in Edit Mode.
  • Something new every levelFifty levels, each unlocking fish, decor or scenery. Daily tasks, a free gift and a login calendar keep things fresh.
  • Plays offline, no sign-upNo email, no password. Your aquarium lives on your iPhone or iPad. Watching a rewarded ad is always your choice.
  • Visit your friendsAdd a friend with a player ID, look at their tanks, leave a like and wipe their glass. No chat, and you can leave at any time.
